Terms of service

These general project notes are not a substitute for a signed project agreement or legal advice.

Before accepting paid work, the operator should review these terms and provide a project-specific written agreement. Legal review has not been independently completed.

Quotes and project scope

Prices shown on this website are introductory estimates only. A project quote depends on requirements, content, functionality and complexity. Work begins only after the customer and VENDERS.CO agree the scope, price, payment terms and any project-specific conditions in writing.

Customer-provided content

Customers should provide accurate content and confirm they have permission to use supplied text, logos, images and other materials. Responsibilities, review deadlines and content preparation will be defined in the project agreement.

Revisions, third-party costs and maintenance

Revision allowances and included work are set out in each accepted quote. Domain registration, hosting, third-party subscriptions, payment fees, premium assets and ongoing maintenance may incur separate costs. Third-party tools remain subject to their own terms and availability.

Timelines and results

Any proposed timeline is confirmed after project assessment and depends on agreed scope, timely customer input and external services. No delivery date, search ranking, traffic, sales or business outcome is guaranteed unless specifically agreed in writing.

Demo work and changes

Industry showcase sites are fictional demonstrations and are not client projects. They may be changed or unavailable. A customer's project must have its own agreed design, deliverables and permissions.

Contact and updates

Verified operator contact details must be added before launch. These notes may be updated as the business and service offering develop.
