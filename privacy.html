What happens to enquiry details?

The current enquiry form runs entirely in your browser. It validates and formats the information you enter but does not transmit it to VENDERS.CO or store it on a server. You decide whether to copy it or open a configured WhatsApp or email action.

If you use WhatsApp or your email application, information is handled by those services under their own terms and privacy practices. A WhatsApp action may place the prepared message into a link; review it before sending.

Cookies, tracking and third parties

This project does not add analytics or advertising trackers. The stylesheet currently references Google Fonts, which may make a connection to Google to retrieve fonts. For a fully self-hosted deployment, download and appropriately license the chosen fonts or use system fonts, then remove that external font request.

Your hosting provider may create server logs according to its own configuration. Confirm the deployed host’s logging, retention and third-party services before launch.

Your choices and questions

Because no enquiry data is collected by this static site, requests about enquiry records should be directed to the relevant service you used. Add a verified VENDERS.CO privacy contact here before launch for questions about any information you share directly with the business.
